Privacy Policy

Freeze & Boil: Water Sort, by ERKBLABS
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Freeze & Boil: Water Sort (“the game”) uses, and why. We keep it short and in plain English.

In short: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts or photos. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google, and uses Google services to find crashes and see how the game is played.

1. Information the game itself keeps

The game saves your progress (stars, open levels, finished worlds) and your settings (sound and vibration) only on your device. We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you use Settings → Reset progress (progress only) or uninstall the game.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (for example model, Android version, language),
  • how you interact with ads.

Where the law allows, ads may be personalised. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for their choice first, using Google’s consent form, and can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID in the phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Ads are never shown in the middle of a level. Learn how Google uses information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Crash reports and play statistics (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to find and fix crashes and to understand how the game is played (for example which levels are hard). This may include crash details, device model, Android version, an app instance ID and game events such as “level won”. It does not include your name or contact details. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you want information deleted, contact us at the email below.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services named above, for the reasons described here.

6. How long information is kept

Progress and settings stay on your device until you reset them or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is kept according to Google’s own policies linked above.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (where available).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Delete everything stored by the game by uninstalling it.
  • Ask us any question, or ask for access to or deletion of your information, by email.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
